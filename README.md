## 1.Organisation overview
### Business name: Psalm coffee
Psalm Coffee is a Christian-inspired coffee shop located in Montana Park, Pretoria. It was established to create more than just a place to enjoy coffee. The coffee shop was founded with the vision of providing a peaceful environment where people can pause, reflect, build meaningful relationships, and experience God’s love in their daily lives.
Psalm Coffee serves premium coffee, freshly prepared food, and provides a welcoming space for Bible studies, business meetings, venue hire, prayer meetings, and small celebrations. One of its unique features is that every coffee cup is served with a Bible verse to encourage and inspire customers.

### Mission:
To serve excellent coffee while creating a peaceful space where people can encounter God’s love, build meaningful relationships, and find rest for their souls.

### Vision:
To become a place where faith, community, and hospitality come together, inspiring lives one cup of coffee at a time.

### Target audience:
Coffee lovers
Families
Students
Business professionals
Church groups
Local residents
Tourists visiting Pretoria
Anyone looking for a peaceful place to work, meet, or relax

## 2.Website goals and objectives
Increase awareness of Psalm Coffee.
 Provide customers with information about the coffee shop.
Promote the menu and services offered.
Advertise venue hire for private events.
Increase customer visits.
Build a professional online presence.
Share the Christian values and welcoming atmosphere of the business.

## 3.Key features and functionality:
The website will allow visitors to:
Learn about Psalm Coffee.
 View services offered.
Find contact details and trading hours.
 View the location.
 Access social media pages.
