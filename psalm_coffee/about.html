Our Story

Psalm Coffee was born from a dream to create more than just a coffee shop. It is a place where people can enjoy great coffee, hear and encounter the presence of God in the midst of everyday life.

The vision behind Psalm Coffee is to create a peaceful environment where every person feels welcome, loved, and seen. Whether someone visits for a morning coffee, quiet devotion time, a meeting with friends, or simply a moment of rest, we want them to leave feeling refreshed in both body and soul.

Every detail inside Psalm Coffee tells a story of God's love and faithfulness -from the Scripture verses on every coffee cup to the prayer wall, symbolic artwork, and spaces designed to encourage reflection, hope, and community.

Psalm Coffee is not about serving exceptional coffee; it is about serving people.

Mission Vision Our Signature
To serve excellent coffee while creating a peaceful space where people can encounter God's love, build meaningful relationships, and find rest for their souls. To become a place where faith, community, and hospitality come together, inspiring lives one cup of coffee at a time. Every coffee is served with a little verse on the cup because we believe encouragement can begin with something as simple as a cup of coffee.
